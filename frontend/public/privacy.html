TheHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

AJIBOYE HUB VENTURES (RC-9776073), operating as "TheHub," ("we," "us," "our") provides a mobile-first booking platform connecting customers with beauty and grooming businesses ("salons," "owners") in Nigeria. This policy explains what personal information we collect through thehubbooking.com and our app, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

We are committed to handling your information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to everyone who uses TheHub — customers booking appointments, and salon owners managing a business listing.

2. Information we collect

From everyone with an account

  • Account details: name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form). If you sign up with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google instead.
  • Profile photo, if you choose to add one.

From customers

  • Booking details: the services you request, the date and time, and your home address if you book a home visit.
  • Location, only when you explicitly tap "Share my location" for a specific booking. This is a one-time share that expires after one hour — we do not track your location continuously or in the background.
  • Messages you send to a salon through in-app chat, and messages a salon sends you.
  • Reviews and ratings you leave after a completed booking.

From salon owners

  • Business details: your salon's name, category, address, hours, bio, and the services and prices you list.
  • Photos and videos of your work that you upload to your salon's gallery.
  • Bank details, only if you choose to connect payouts for a feature that requires it — this is stored securely and is never shown to customers.

What we do not collect

TheHub does not process payments for bookings. Customers pay salons directly, in person. We do not collect or store your card details, bank account numbers (as a customer), or any payment information for booking a service.

Automatically collected information

Like most apps, our servers log basic technical information (such as IP address and request timestamps) for security purposes — for example, to detect and block abuse. We do not use this to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect customers with salons and process bookings
  • Enable in-app messaging between customers and salons
  • Verify check-ins and track loyalty visits at a salon you've booked with
  • Send booking-related notifications (confirmations, reminders, status updates) by email or in-app notification
  • Improve the reliability and security of the app
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share information with

  • The salon you book with sees your name, contact details, booking details, and (if you share it) your location for that booking — this is necessary for them to provide the service.
  • Service providers who help us run TheHub, under contractual obligations to protect your data:
  • Cloudinary — stores and delivers photos and videos uploaded to the app
  • Resend — sends transactional emails (booking confirmations, password resets)
  • Render — hosts our backend server and database
  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google
  • We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your account and booking information for as long as your account is active, so you can access your booking history. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep certain records for legal or security reasons.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for location sharing at any time (simply don't tap "Share my location")
  • Object to how your information is being used

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@thehubbooking.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted password storage, secure connections (HTTPS), and access controls that ensure only you and the salons you've booked with can see your booking details.

No system is 100% secure, but we take this seriously and continuously work to improve it.

8. Children

TheHub is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TheHub grows. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
